Local is free. Hosting is optional.

The CLI and SDK have no account requirement. This hosted beta allows 100 active publications and 256 MiB per account, with a 32 MiB archive limit. Teams can share ownership with writer and reader roles. Paid plans are not yet enabled.

Does this transfer my whole machine or repository?

No. It transfers the supported conversation context. Local files, live processes, credentials, and the source repository are not automatically uploaded. Prepare the destination workspace separately.

Can I revoke a link?

Yes. New requests stop immediately. Revocation cannot recall copies someone already downloaded or independently published.

Will all tools resume identically?

Different tools support different context. Preview the compatibility warnings with --dry-run before importing.
